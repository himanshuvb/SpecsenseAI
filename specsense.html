SpecSense AI: Problem Statement

Overview

In digital commerce and service ecosystems, matching users to the right products is a critical driver of engagement and retention.

While traditional recommender systems rely heavily on rigid filters or historical behavior, they often fail to capture the real-time context and intent behind a user's need.

SpecSense AI bridges this gap by leveraging Generative AI to process a user's natural language preferences and context and intelligently match them to the most suitable product(s) from a company-provided catalog.

Instead of overwhelming users with irrelevant choices, it offers context-aware, explainable recommendations backed by structured product features and customer profile metadata.

This solution is especially valuable for enterprises that already possess clean product and customer datasets and want to enhance personalization without redesigning their entire platform logic.

Problem Context

Most recommendation systems struggle to support:

  • Flexible Input: Users can't express needs like "I want something light, with great battery and under ₹30k".
  • Transparent Recommendations: Why a product was recommended isn't made clear.
  • Hybrid Filtering: Combining structured product specs with soft preferences such as "good in low light" or "eco-friendly".

Companies have the data but lack the intelligence layer to connect the dots using natural language understanding, real-time context, and personalized matching.

This leads to:

  • Customer indecision or drop-offs
  • Frustration due to irrelevant recommendations
  • Underutilization of product diversity

Why It Matters for Enterprises

This system will help businesses:

  • Deliver human-like, conversational product matching
  • Make product discovery faster and more trusted
  • Convert vague user intent into concrete decisions
  • Reduce cognitive load on customers

Data

  • Product Catalog: Structured CSV/JSON with name, specs, category, description
  • User Profile: Age, budget, context tags, product history

What SpecSense AI Does

SpecSense AI is a modular system that:

  1. Understands free-form product preference queries
    Example: "Need a lightweight laptop under ₹30k for travel and battery life"
  2. Extracts structured constraints such as budget, weight and purpose, along with soft preferences such as long battery life and travel-friendly.
  3. Matches user intent with structured catalog data
  4. Generates explainable product recommendations, clearly breaking down:
    • What criteria were met
    • Where trade-offs exist
    • What soft preferences were partially fulfilled
  5. Highlights gaps if no perfect match is found, and suggests the closest alternatives with rationale.
  6. Enriches results with real-world insights, such as customer review snippets, availability status and current offers.
  7. Evaluates recommendation quality, explainability and user satisfaction for continuous improvement.

User Flow

  1. User Query Input (Natural Language)
    "Looking for a lightweight laptop under ₹30k, good for coding and battery backup"
  2. Intent Understanding & Constraint Extraction
    • Hard specs: Budget, weight, use-case keywords
    • Soft needs: "good for coding", "battery backup"
  3. Matching Engine
    • Match structured data such as RAM and battery life
    • Soft match using LLMs, for example associating "good for coding" with CPU, RAM and reviews
  4. Recommendation + Transparency Layer

    For each product, show:

    • Hard matches
    • Soft matches
    • Trade-offs
    • Unmet needs
  5. Gap Detection Layer (if needed)
    "No products meet all criteria. Showing best alternatives…"
  6. Enrichment with Real-World Insights
    • Add relevant customer review snippets
    • Mention availability/offers if data exists
  7. Evaluation Layer
    • Track quality of match, correctness of rationale, and explainability.

Technical Requirements

  • Python 3.12+
  • Gemini API (Google Generative AI) or OpenAI API (optional)
  • Pandas (for parsing and structuring reviews)
  • Streamlit (for interactive UI)
  • Python-dotenv (for environment configs)
  • Pydantic (for schema validation)
